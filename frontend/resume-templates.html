Resume Writing Tips

Expert advice to make your resume stand out

Tailor for Each Job

Customize your resume for each application by highlighting relevant skills and experiences that match the job requirements.

Quantify Achievements

Use numbers and percentages to demonstrate your impact. For example, "Increased sales by 25%" is more compelling than "Increased sales."

Optimize for ATS

Use relevant keywords from the job posting and avoid complex formatting to ensure your resume passes through applicant tracking systems.

Keep It Concise

Aim for 1-2 pages maximum. Focus on your most relevant and recent experiences that demonstrate your qualifications for the role.
